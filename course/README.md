# Claude and AI Mastery Course

Not part of the Revenue Boost mission (see `CLAUDE.md`); kept in `course/` at the owner's request.

## Goal
Take someone with no background to expert-level use of LLMs, technically and practically.

## Yardstick
Understand anything discussed about LLMs on Reddit, both the technical and the practical side.

## Method
- One learner (the course owner) is the test audience. If they master a topic, it becomes a lesson for everyone else.
- Socratic: questions first, then build, then name the concept. Not a linear syllabus.
- Skill plus knowledge: every topic ends with something built or run.
- Topics are picked from what is current on Reddit and in the Claude docs, then recorded in `progress.md`.

## Files
- `progress.md`: what is known, what is shaky, what is next.
- `lessons/`: one note per session, written after the session.
